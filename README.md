# Clearpath Finance

A personal finance tracker: one place to see what you own, what you owe, and what you traded.

## Planned features

- **Assets and accounts.** Track holdings across brokerage, exchange, bank and cash accounts, with current value and allocation.
- **Trades.** Log buys, sells and fees, with cost basis and realized and unrealized profit and loss per position.
- **Debt payoff.** Plan payoff with the avalanche (highest rate first) or snowball (smallest balance first) method, and see the payoff date and total interest for each.
- **Cash flow.** Income and spending by category, plus a monthly view of net worth over time.
- **Import.** CSV import from common exchanges and banks, so nothing has to be typed in by hand.

## Status

Early stage: scope and data model first. Contributions and issues welcome.
